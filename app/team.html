Open Positions

Internship

We offer internships online or in-person for instance via the Humboldt Internship Program.

We support ERASMUS student internships.

Bachelor Thesis

Undergraduate (Bachelor) students are always welcome. Projects can take place in Lübeck or Berlin.

Master Thesis

Graduate (Master) students are always welcome. Projects can take place in Lübeck or Berlin.

PhD

We have a fully funded open position available as PhD in Lübeck as a Research Software Engineer (RSE) or Informatician.

We also supervise doctoral projects if you have a fellowship or similar funding source.

PostDoc

We have a fully funded open position available as PostDoc in Lübeck as a Research Software Engineer (RSE) or Informatician.

We also supervise postdoctoral projects if you have a fellowship or similar funding source.

We offer projects on the following topics

Digital Twins

Physiologically based models that mirror individual patients, from molecule to whole body.

AI

Mechanistic modeling meets machine learning for predictive, personalized medicine.

Digital Pathology

AI-driven analysis of whole-slide histology to quantify liver structure and disease at scale.

Pharmacometrics

Physiologically based pharmacokinetic/ pharmacodynamic models for precision dosing.

Open & FAIR

Reproducible, standardized models and data that the community can build on.

Interested in joining the group? Contact Prof. Matthias König.
