# Release notes for livermetabolism-site 0.12.4

A patch release that shows the amount of every grant on the research page, and adds a new award, a published paper, a poster, a software package and three news items.

## Features
- every funding card on the research page shows the grant's total, formatted for the page's language (`€3,825,000` in English, `3.825.000 €` in German), plus the group's share when that is only part of the total (#91). A grant without an amount leaves the line out rather than showing 0. `llms-full.txt` carries the same amount line, and its German half now names the funding role in German

## News
- Anthropic's AI for Science program funds AI-Ready PK-DB (#88, #92): an MCP server that lets AI agents navigate PK-DB from a clinical study to its concentration-time profiles and on to an executable PBPK model. The award joins the funding list and has its own news item
- the Llemy paper is published in Bioinformatics Advances (#84) and replaces its bioRxiv preprint on the publications page, with the journal PDF
- the PK-DB 2.0 poster from the Open Systems Pharmacology Community Conference 2026 in Beerse, Belgium, joins the posters, with a news item and a photo from the poster session (#93)
- `pkpdbib` joins the software packages and the GitHub statistics (#94). It is a Python library for PK/PD literature and bibliographies, from retrieving the PDFs of a Zotero export to programmatic access to Zotero libraries, and supports the literature curation of PK-DB
- Mariia Myshkina is a member of the 2027-28 Young Scientists Committee of VPH, the Society for In Silico Medicine (#94)

## Development
- the OpenAlex test derives its expected DOI list from `data/publications.yml` instead of pinning the first DOI and the count, so adding a publication no longer breaks the build (#84)
- dependency updates: Astro 7.3.5, `@astrojs/vue` 7.0.3, Vue 3.5.43, js-yaml 5.4.2, Vitest 5.0.2, `@vue/test-utils` 2.5.1, pandas 3.0.6, markdown 3.11 and `astral-sh/setup-uv` 10.2.0 (#82, #83, #85, #86, #87)
- Vitest runs 700 tests, Python validation 38 tests, and Playwright 85 browser tests
