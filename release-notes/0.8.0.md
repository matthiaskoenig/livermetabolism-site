# Release notes for livermetabolism-site 0.8.0

We are pleased to release the next version of the König Lab website, https://livermetabolism.com. This release adds a network page: an interactive graph of the people, publications, projects, and software of the lab, filterable by research area, that leads from every node to the item on the site.

## Features
- a new "Network" page (`/network/`, in the navbar and linked from every research-area section of the homepage) draws the lab as a force-directed graph: people with their photos, projects and software with their thumbnails, and publications as dots coloured by research area and sized by citation count; links follow authorship and project and software membership
- hovering a node shows its name and a detail line (role, year and journal, project title, tool name) and a paper's citation count; clicking a person, paper, project, or tool opens it where it lives on the site (team modal, publication row, project modal, software card), and the browser's back button returns to the graph
- the "All" button and one per research area filter the graph to that area — its publications, projects, and software, and everybody who co-authored one of its papers — and a `?topic=<area>` link arrives with that filter applied; the nodes that stay keep their places instead of being re-arranged; the legend toggles node types; drag to pan, buttons zoom and reset the view

## Performance
- the graph page carries its rows in the page (about 32 KB gzipped) and loads the graph renderer together with the chart bundle the site already ships; node images are 96 px thumbnails generated once (97 files, 428 KB in total) and served as static assets
- filtering by research area or resizing the window keeps the node positions instead of restarting the layout, so the page stays calm on phones where the address bar changes the viewport height while scrolling

## Fixes
- the `Network` button on the homepage sections and the `active` state of the navbar entry follow the existing patterns; the wheel over the graph scrolls the page instead of zooming it

## Development
- `scripts/graph-thumbs.ts` (`npm run graph:thumbs`) writes the circular people thumbnails (with a white ring) and the square project and software thumbnails into `public/assets/image/graph/` with `sharp` — 97 files, 428 KB; the output is committed and regenerated by hand when images change
- `site/lib/graphRows.ts` derives the nodes and links from the YAML data and the citation snapshot at build time (pure, tested against the real data: 208 nodes, 306 links); `site/lib/networkOptions.ts` builds the ECharts graph option and filters the rows by research area (`filterRows()`, richText tooltips) without touching the DOM; `GraphChart` joins the registered ECharts components
- Vitest runs 354 tests, Playwright 33 (the network page renders the canvas and the research-area filter, `?topic=` pre-selects one, zero console errors on every page)
- the design spec and the plan are in `docs/superpowers/`; `CLAUDE.md` documents the graph rows, the thumbnails, and the island
