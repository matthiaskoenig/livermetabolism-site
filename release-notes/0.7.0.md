# Release notes for livermetabolism-site 0.7.0

We are pleased to release the next version of the König Lab website, https://livermetabolism.com. This release adds citation counts to every publication, a publications-over-time chart, and an at-a-glance strip on the homepage, and keeps every number on the site at most a day old with a daily rebuild.

## Features
- every publication with a DOI shows how often it has been cited, from [OpenAlex](https://openalex.org/), as a badge linking to the OpenAlex record, and an "open access" badge when the paper is openly available; the counts are refreshed daily and updated in the browser like the GitHub and Scholar data
- a Year / Most cited toggle next to the tag filter re-orders the publication list; `?order=cited` links open the most-cited view, and the research-area filter works in both orders
- a "Publications over time" chart above the list shows papers per year stacked by research area in the site's tag colours, with a switch to a split by publication status; clicking a segment applies that research-area filter to the list, clicking a year jumps to it
- the homepage opens with six figures under the title: publications, citations, h-index, team (members and alumni), software (packages and stars), and funded projects, each linking to its page
- the site is rebuilt and deployed every morning after the data snapshots are refreshed, so build-time numbers such as the homepage strip never fall more than a day behind

## Security
- OpenAlex is contacted only by the daily workflow, never by a visitor's browser; the only strings taken from its answers are the work identifier, validated against the `W<digits>` pattern before it becomes a link, and the open-access status, rendered as text
- the ordering toggle moves the existing rows rather than rendering a second copy, and the badge updater writes text, `href`, and `hidden` only; the Content-Security-Policy is unchanged

## Performance
- the homepage strip is rendered at build time and ships no script; the homepage HTML grows by 0.2 KB gzipped
- the publications page grows from 102 to 106 KB gzipped for 87 badge groups and the chart section; the chart reuses the ECharts bundle the page already loads for the citation charts

## Fixes
- OpenAlex sometimes holds two records for one DOI; the fetch requests a full page per batch, keeps the more-cited record, and warns instead of silently dropping a paper
- the publication list is sorted by year at build time, so the most-cited order breaks ties by year regardless of the order in `publications.yml`
- the "updated …" notes of the three live-data sections share one refresh routine, so each note's machine-readable date stays in step with its text
- the order and tag-filter buttons expose their state with `aria-pressed`

## Development
- `scripts/fetch-citations.ts` collects the 86 unique DOIs of `data/publications.yml`, asks OpenAlex in batches with retries, and writes `citations.json` to the `github-data` branch as the third daily snapshot (`site/lib/citationsSchema.ts` is its strict schema; a contact address for OpenAlex's polite pool comes from the optional repository variable `OPENALEX_MAILTO`)
- `.github/workflows/site.yml` gains a daily schedule at 06:00 UTC; the data workflow runs at 05:00 UTC
- Vitest runs 306 tests (77 new: DOI normalisation, batching, retries, the OpenAlex transform against a recorded response, the schema, the badge patcher, the ordering logic on a DOM fixture, the chart rows, the homepage figures); Playwright runs 30 tests (badges, ordering, chart, strip, zero console errors on every page)
- the design spec and the plan are in `docs/superpowers/`; `CLAUDE.md` and `README.md` describe the third snapshot, the daily deploy, and the ordering/tag-filter interplay
