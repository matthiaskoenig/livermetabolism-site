# Release notes for livermetabolism-site 0.8.1

A patch release that makes the network page (`/network/`, new in 0.8.0) easier to read and to handle.

## Features
- projects and software are drawn as shapes in their category colour — projects as rounded squares, software as diamonds — and only people carry their photo; publications stay dots in their research area's colour
- every node is sized by how many people, papers, projects and tools it connects to, counted inside the view being drawn, so a research area re-sizes its own network; a publication's citation count is in its tooltip
- choosing a research area re-arranges the graph for the remaining nodes and fits it to the canvas, while resizing the window leaves the arrangement alone
- only connected nodes are drawn, so the graph is always a network: a research area shows its connected network, and 23 people without a publication, project, or software entry of their own are left out of the full view
- dragging a node now moves that node instead of panning the whole graph; dragging the background pans, anywhere on the canvas, and the buttons zoom and reset the view

## Development
- the graph thumbnails are people only (61 files, 252 KB): projects, software, and research areas need no image any more, so `public/assets/image/graph/projects/`, `software/` and `topics/` are gone and `scripts/graph-thumbs.ts` renders the people photos alone
- `filterRows()` in `site/lib/networkOptions.ts` narrows the rows to one research area and drops the nodes left without a link; the node sizes come from the degree of that filtered view (`degrees()`, `symbolSize()`), and `networkOption(rows, topic, { relayout })` decides whether a render re-runs the force layout (a filter) or keeps every position (a resize), which `useChart`'s per-render `notMerge` predicate follows
- `enableNodeDragging()` in `site/components/useChart.ts` switches ECharts' roaming off while a node is dragged: for a node drawn as an image symbol the pointer target is the inner image, which is not draggable, so the roam controller used to pan the view instead
- Vitest runs 354 tests, Playwright 34 (the new one drags a node and asserts that the rest of the canvas stays put)
