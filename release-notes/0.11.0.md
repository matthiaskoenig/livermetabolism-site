# Release notes for livermetabolism-site 0.11.0

We are pleased to release the next version of the König Lab website, https://livermetabolism.com. This release makes the site available in US English and German, with a language switch in the navbar.

## Features
- the site is now available in US English (the default, at the root) and German (under `/de/`), with a language switch in the navbar that keeps the current page and any open detail modal
- every page renders from one source file under `site/pages/[...locale]/`, so the two language trees cannot drift apart from each other
- the group's own prose, research areas, people, projects, software, funding, news, teaching, meetings, editors, and activities, is translated. Bibliographic records (publications, presentations, posters, abstracts) keep their English text, since a paper's title is how it is cited and found
- the legal pages (impressum, privacy) keep German as their source, since that version is the legally binding one, and their English rendering says so
- `llms.txt` and `llms-full.txt` now carry both languages, and every page carries `hreflang` alternates to the other language and to itself as `x-default`

## Development
- roughly 270 UI strings live in a typed catalog; a German key that goes missing is a build error, not an English string leaking onto a German page
- German lives in generated catalogs under `i18n/de/` and is never hand-edited. `npm run i18n:check` compares every German entry against a hash of its English source and fails CI when the two drift apart
- preparing the translation also surfaced and fixed a number of defects in the English content: a news teaser that described the wrong drug, a published paper that was labelled as a preprint, several malformed links and tags, and a misspelled colleague's name
- Vitest runs 570 tests, Python validation 36 tests, and Playwright 67 browser tests, including a loop over every German page and a check that every `hreflang` alternate resolves
