# Release notes for livermetabolism-site 0.12.3

A patch release that makes the colours of the network graph readable and lets a person carry research areas of their own.

## Fixes
- the network graph colours every node by its type alone, and its legend shows exactly that (#77). A publication used to take the colour of its research area, and that palette shares its blue and orange with the People and Projects colours, so the legend called blue "People" while the blue circles were papers, and the red and green ones had no legend entry at all. Every node is now outlined in the colour of its type and filled with a light tint of it; the research areas filter the graph and no longer colour it
- the legend draws each entry as the symbol of its nodes, in their outline and fill: a circle for people and publications, a rounded square for projects, a diamond for software. It is larger than before and sits on a translucent background, so no edge of the graph runs through its text
- a photo cannot be outlined by the chart library, so the ring of a person node is part of the thumbnail; the 61 photo thumbnails are regenerated in the people colour

## Features
- a person can carry research-area tags in `data/people.yml` (#78). Until now a person's areas were derived from their publications alone, which left out anyone working in an area before a paper says so; Michelle and Shubhankar are tagged Digital Pathology this way. The team page filter and the network graph both use the person's own tags plus those of their publications

## Development
- the people colour of the thumbnails and of the graph are kept equal by a test, because the thumbnail script runs under plain Node and cannot import the graph's module
- Vitest runs 692 tests, Python validation 38 tests, and Playwright 85 browser tests
