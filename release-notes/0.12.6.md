# Release notes for livermetabolism-site 0.12.6

A patch release that announces the Open Science Award 2026 of the University of Lübeck, updates two publications, and makes continuous integration lighter and faster.

## News
- news: the jury of the Open Science Award of the University of Lübeck selected our open, reproducible digital twin of hydrochlorothiazide pharmacokinetics and pharmacodynamics for the 2026 award, in English and German (#107)
- the FAIR assessment paper carries its DOI 10.1038/s41540-026-00828-7 in npj Systems Biology and Applications (#102)
- the standard-GEM preprint is the revised bioRxiv v2 of 2026-10-05, with its new title, authors, abstract and PDF; it keeps one entry under the same DOI, so the paper is not counted twice (#103)

## Development
- continuous integration does less work (#104): every workflow cancels a superseded run, the site build caches the Python interpreter and the Playwright browser, and dependabot proposes its updates monthly instead of weekly. The daily data snapshot and the daily rebuild stay
- the Playwright tests run in parallel, four workers in CI (85 tests in 36 s instead of 90 s locally); the real-data Python tests load the database once, and every CI job has a timeout (#104)
- the design documents and plans of coding agents are removed from the repository and gitignored (#104)
- dependency updates: astro 7.3.6, js-yaml 5.4.3, vitest 5.0.3, vue-tsc 3.3.12, sharp 0.35.5 and source-map-js 1.2.2 (#98, #101, #105, #106)
- Vitest runs 700 tests, Python validation 38 tests, and Playwright 85 browser tests
